## Real demo vs visual concept

The 13 SVG thumbnails in assets/previews are design *concepts*, not screenshots of running apps. To publish real app demos without publishing code, host compiled frontend/backend on an appropriate server and share a safe visitor URL with demo-only/test data. Never publicly expose admin, patient, financial, private customer, internal infrastructure, test credentials or secrets.

## Contributions

GitHub's native calendar is the authoritative contribution graph. Enable **Private contributions** from your profile > Contribution settings to display anonymous aggregate activity from private repositories (without exposing names or code). You cannot truthfully fill missing calendar cells or backdate work that didn't happen.
